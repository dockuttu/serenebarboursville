Why Kanawha Valley patients drive to Serene

Patients from Charleston, South Charleston, St. Albans and Nitro make the I-64 trip for three reasons: a physician does the work, the prices are published (and matched), and the advanced treatments — Ultherapy, Alma Hybrid, Morpheus-style RF, Sculptra — are all in one place.

Serene Med Spa is owned by Dr. Shweta Arora (board-certified anesthesiology, certified in aesthetic medicine) and Dr. Robin Arora (board-certified internal medicine), who see every patient with Stephanie Welker, FNP-BC. We’re an Allergan Platinum partner, a Merz Bronze preferred practice, an Ultherapy PRIME provider, and an Alma and Cartessa device practice.

That means the full menu: Botox ($10/unit), the whole Juvéderm family ($500/syringe), Kybella, Sculptra, Ultherapy (full face $2,200), Alma Hybrid laser resurfacing, Opus Plasma, Soprano laser hair removal, SKNLAB and HydraFacial ($150), medical weight loss and Biöte hormones — with every price on our price list and a Tri-State price match on injectables.

The drive is a straight run: I-64 west from Charleston through Hurricane and Milton to exit 20 (Barboursville / Huntington Mall), about 45 minutes; from Hurricane or Teays Valley it’s closer to 20. Tell us you’re coming from the Kanawha Valley and we’ll book your consultation and treatment together so one trip covers both. Free parking at the door.
