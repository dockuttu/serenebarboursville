A facial that adapts to your skin

SKNLAB, by Cartessa Aesthetics, is a six-handpiece facial platform. Its Aquis aqua-delivery handpiece does what a HydraFacial does — deep-cleans, exfoliates, extracts and infuses hydrating solutions — and the other five bring proven energies into the same visit: ultrasonic prep, hot and cold therapy, electroporation for serum delivery, microcurrent toning, and lymphatic massage.

At Serene Med Spa in Barboursville, our providers start with a quick skin assessment (we also have VISIA imaging on site) and then sequence the handpieces around what your skin needs that day: more extraction and cold therapy for congested, breakout-prone skin; more electroporation and microcurrent for dull or early-aging skin; more hydration and lymphatic drainage before an event. A full SKNLAB facial takes 30–40 minutes and feels like a hydrating facial with light suction and a few new sensations from the energy handpieces — nothing painful, and you leave with makeup-ready skin. It is also an excellent hydrating step between Alma Hybrid, Opus Plasma or laser sessions. Individual results vary.

Good to Know

SKNLAB FAQ

SKNLAB is a six-handpiece facial device from Cartessa Aesthetics that combines aqua-dermabrasion with five energy-based handpieces (ultrasonic, hot/cold, electroporation, microcurrent and lymphatic massage), so each facial can be customized to your skin.

The Aquis handpiece performs the same cleanse-exfoliate-extract-hydrate steps as a HydraFacial. SKNLAB then adds the energy handpieces, so your provider can also calm inflammation, push serums deeper, tone muscles, and drain puffiness in the same visit. Both are $150 at Serene Barboursville.

A comprehensive SKNLAB facial takes about 30 to 40 minutes.

Aqua-delivery feels hydrating with some gentle suction that should never pull the skin. The energy handpieces add warmth, cooling, light vibration or a mild tingle — your provider explains each step before it starts.

Right away. The cleansing and infusion leave skin hydrated and more radiant immediately; smaller-looking pores and a toned feel are common. Other benefits build over a series.

A custom facial once a month is the usual recommendation. Your provider will tailor frequency to your skin and any laser or peel series you are doing.

We postpone treatment for pregnancy or nursing, active skin conditions in the area (rash, eczema, sunburn, cold sores, active rosacea or severe acne), current or past skin cancer in the area, and recent resurfacing. Under 18 needs parental consent.
