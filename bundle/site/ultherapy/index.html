Lift and tighten — without surgery

Ultherapy is the gold standard in non-invasive lifting. Using micro-focused ultrasound, it stimulates your body’s own collagen deep beneath the surface to lift the brow, chin, and neck and smooth the décolleté.

Now available at our Barboursville office serving Huntington and the Tri-State, Ultherapy uses ultrasound imaging so your provider can actually see the layers of tissue being treated — delivering energy precisely where it does the most good. Most people need just one session, with no downtime, and results that develop gradually over two to three months and can last a year or more.
