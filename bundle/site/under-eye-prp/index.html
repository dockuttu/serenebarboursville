A natural approach to tired-looking eyes

The under-eye area is thin, delicate and often the first place to show fatigue. PRP (platelet-rich plasma) and PRF (platelet-rich fibrin) concentrate the growth factors in your own blood and place them exactly where the skin needs support — building collagen and improving texture, tone and thickness over time.

At Serene Med Spa in Barboursville, we offer both PRP and PRF for the under-eyes. PRF is spun more slowly and without anticoagulant, so it forms a soft fibrin scaffold that releases growth factors gradually — many patients like it for a longer, more sustained effect. Neither adds volume the way filler does, which is exactly why it suits patients who want a refreshed look without the risk of puffiness. Results build over a series of sessions and individual results vary.

The Experience

What to Expect

Consultation

We assess your under-eye concern and decide whether PRP, PRF, filler or a combination will give the most natural result.

Treatment

A small blood draw is spun in-office; after numbing, the PRP or PRF is placed beneath the under-eye skin with a fine cannula or needle.

Results

Skin quality improves gradually over 4–8 weeks as collagen builds. Most patients see the best result after a series of 2–3 sessions.

Maintenance

A maintenance session every 6–12 months helps sustain the improvement.

Good to Know

Under-Eye PRP / PRF FAQ

Both come from your own blood. PRP is spun faster and is a liquid concentrate of platelets; PRF is spun slower without additives, forming a gel-like fibrin matrix that releases growth factors more gradually. We’ll recommend the right one for you.

PRP and PRF improve skin quality rather than adding volume. For true hollowing, we may pair them with under-eye filler or Skinvive.

Most patients do a series of 2–3 sessions spaced about a month apart, then maintain every 6–12 months.

Expect mild swelling and possible bruising for a few days. Most people return to normal activities right away.

Under-eye PRP is $400 and PRF is $600 per session. You can book online and your injector will confirm the plan at your visit.
